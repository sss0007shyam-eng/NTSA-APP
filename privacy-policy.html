Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how NTSA, operated by SSS Enterprises ("we", "us"), collects, uses and protects your information when you use the NTSA mobile app and website.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number, name and email (if you give it).
  • Login: we use Google Firebase to send and check a one-time password (OTP) sent to your phone.
  • Delivery details: your address, pin code and phone number, so the order reaches you.
  • Orders and payments: items you buy, order history, and payment status. We do not store your full card number. Payments are handled by our payment partner.
  • Device permissions: notifications (to send order updates), microphone (only for voice search), camera or photos (only when you search by photo or upload a photo, for example a return or a problem report).
  • Support messages: messages you send to our support team about an order.
  • Device identifiers: a push-notification token, so we can send you order updates.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and log you in.
  • To process, deliver and track your orders.
  • To send order and delivery notifications, and support replies.
  • To handle returns, refunds and complaints.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the app secure.

3. Sharing your information

We share only what is needed:

  • Sellers: the name, delivery address and contact needed to deliver their items.
  • Courier partners: the delivery details needed to deliver your order and share tracking updates.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (login and notifications), cloud storage for images, and our payment partner.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and limit access to people who need it to do their job.

5. Your choices

  • You can turn off notifications, microphone or camera access in your phone settings at any time.
  • You can ask us to correct your information.
  • You can delete your account from within the app, or by writing to us at the email below. Some order records may be kept for legal and tax reasons.

6. Children

NTSA is not meant for children under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be shown in the app.
