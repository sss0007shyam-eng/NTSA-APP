Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Return windows differ by product and seller. The window shown on each product page and in your order is the one that applies.

1. Eligible for return

  • Item is damaged, defective, or not as described.
  • Wrong item or wrong size/colour delivered.
  • Item is missing from the package.

Report the problem in the app within [X] days of delivery, with clear photos of the item and the packaging.

2. Not eligible for return

  • Items marked non-returnable on the product page (for example, personal-care or hygiene products once opened).
  • Items used, washed or damaged after delivery.
  • Items without original tags, packaging or accessories.
  • Change of mind, where the product's policy does not allow it.

3. How to request a return

  1. Open the order in the app and tap Report a problem.
  2. Choose the reason and add photos.
  3. Our support team reviews the request and contacts you.
  4. If approved, a pickup is arranged, or you are asked to ship the item back as instructed.

4. Refunds

  • Refunds are issued after the returned item is received and checked, or after the seller confirms the problem.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, or to your NTSA wallet if agreed. Bank processing can take [X] working days.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable, unless the return is due to our or the seller's error.

5. Cancellations

An order can be cancelled before it is shipped. Once shipped, it must be returned under the rules above.
