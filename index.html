FAQ

Questions people ask us

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

How do I create an account?

Open the app, enter your mobile number and the OTP we send you. That's it. There is no separate sign-up form.

How do I track my order?

Open My Orders and tap your order. You'll see the courier name, the tracking number, and every step with its time. Tap the tracking link to see it on the courier's own site too.

My order says delivered but I didn't get it. What now?

Open the order, tap Problem with this order? and choose "Not received". Our support team checks with the courier and the seller and replies to you in the app.

What if my item is damaged or wrong?

Report it in the app within the return window shown on the product page. Add clear photos of the item and the packaging. We'll arrange a return or replacement as applicable.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are issued after the returned item is received and checked, or after the seller confirms the problem. Bank time depends on your bank. The full rule is in our Refund Policy.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, before it is shipped. Once shipped, it goes through the return process.

Who is selling the products?

Each product shows its seller. NTSA is the platform. The seller is responsible for the product, and we help when something goes wrong.

Is my phone number and data safe?

We use your number only to log you in, send OTPs and deliver your orders. Read the details in our Privacy Policy, including how to delete your account.

Which permissions does the app ask for?

Notifications (for order updates), microphone (only for voice search), and camera or photos (only for photo search or when you report a problem with pictures). You can turn these off in your phone settings.
